# Privacy Policy - JobCare Voice

**Last Updated:** July 21, 2026

## 1. Introduction

JobCare Voice ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users ("you" or "your"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web platform (collectively, the "Platform").

By using JobCare Voice, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our Platform.

JobCare Voice complies with the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 under the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information You Provide

When you register, create a profile, or use our services, we may collect:

- **Identity Information**: Full name, date of birth, gender
- **Contact Information**: Email address, phone number, residential address
- **Professional Information**: Work experience, education, skills, certifications, employment history, references
- **Resume Data**: Documents, files, voice recordings, and video introductions you upload
- **Account Credentials**: Username, password, and authentication data
- **Payment Information**: When making payments, we collect payment method details (processed through Razorpay, we do not store complete payment card information)
- **Communication Data**: Messages sent through our chat platform, support inquiries, and feedback

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Platform, we may automatically collect:

- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, mobile network information
- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, features used, time spent, interactions, search queries
- **Location Data**: Precise GPS location (when enabled) for nearby job recommendations; approximate location derived from IP address
- **Voice Data**: Voice recordings when you use voice search, voice commands, or voice resume features
- **Log Data**: IP address, browser type, referring URLs, access times, and diagnostic data
- **Performance Data**: Crash reports, app performance metrics, and feature usage statistics

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from:

- **Google Sign-In**: Profile information (name, email, profile picture) when you use Google authentication
- **Sarvam AI**: Processed transcription and synthesis data from voice interactions
- **Firebase**: Push notification tokens, analytics data, crash reports
- **Employers**: Feedback about your job applications and interviews

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. We may retain certain information for legitimate business purposes or as required by law.

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Account information | Until account deletion + 90 days |
| Job applications | 2 years after application |
| Voice recordings | 12 months after recording |
| Payment records | 6 years (tax compliance) |
| Usage analytics | 24 months |
| Chat messages | Until account deletion |
| Resume documents | Until account deletion |

After the retention period, your data will be securely deleted or anonymized.

## 6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security practices and procedures to protect your personal information:

- **Encryption**: All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3; sensitive data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
- **Access Control**: Strict role-based access controls and authentication requirements
- **Regular Audits**: Periodic security assessments and penetration testing
- **Secure Storage**: Data stored in secure, access-controlled environments
- **Employee Training**: Regular security awareness training for all employees
- **Incident Response**: Documented incident response plan for data breaches

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
